### User Story E07-S04:

- **Summary:** Send the weekly client report automatically, under my agency's brand

**Epic:** E07 — Agency Workspace & Multi-Client Reporting
**Phase:** 3
**Source:** Concept note §4 P3 (exportable periodic reports), §3 (replacing spreadsheet-and-screenshot reporting)

#### Use Case:
- **As an** agency account manager who rebuilds the same deck for each client every Friday
- **I want to** schedule a recurring branded report per client that generates and sends itself
- **so that** I spend my Friday interpreting the numbers for my client instead of copying them into
  a slide

#### Acceptance Criteria:
- **Scenario:** Manager schedules a weekly Friday report for one client
- **Given:** my agency has an active grant on that client's entities
- **and Given:** the report builder lets me choose entities, period, sections, and recipients, and
  apply my agency's logo and colours
- **and Given:** the report carries the account-type scope and coverage-gap disclosure from E05-S03
  and E05-S08
- **and Given:** I can preview the report before scheduling it
- **and Given:** generation re-checks my grant at send time and skips any entity I no longer have
  access to
- **When:** I schedule the report for every Friday at 09:00
- **Then:** the branded report generates and is sent to the chosen recipients each Friday, containing
  only the entities my grant still covers at that moment

#### Notes
- Re-checking grants at send time rather than at schedule time is what stops a revoked client's
  data going out in next week's email (E07-S03).
- Reuses the digest delivery infrastructure from E08-S01 rather than a second mail path.

#### Out of scope
- Editable narrative commentary generated by AI inside the report.
- Client-side report portals with logins.
